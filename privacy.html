Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

The short version: Brainsmith has no ads and no tracking, and you can play without signing up. We only keep what the game needs to work: the name you type, the words you play and the games you're in. If you choose to sign in, we also keep your email so your games follow you.

What we collect

  • The name you type. It can be a nickname. Other players in your game see it.
  • Your game activity. The words you play, emoji reactions, votes, and when they happened. Everyone in that game can see them.
  • A random player ID. When you open Brainsmith, your phone gets an anonymous ID so the game knows which seat is yours. If you never sign in, it isn't tied to your email, phone number or real identity.
  • Your email, only if you sign in. Signing in is optional. If you sign in with Google or with an email and password, we keep your email address so you can get back into your account on another phone. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase; we never see them. Your email is never shown to other players.
  • Alerts, only if you turn them on. Your phone gives us an address for sending game alerts (your turn, nudges, wins). Turning alerts off or deleting your account removes it.
  • Reports and blocks. If you report a player, we keep the report (their name, their words in that game, your reason and note) so we can look into it. If you block a player, that list is saved to your account so it follows you.

We don't ask for your phone number, contacts, location or photos, and we don't collect payment details.

What stays on your phone

Brainsmith saves a few things in your browser so it works smoothly: your name, the list of games you're in, who you've blocked, whether sound is on, and whether you've seen How to Play. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Who handles the data

Games are stored with Google Firebase, which runs the game's database, sign-in, alerts and website hosting. Fonts come from Google Fonts. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us for sign-in only. Like any website, these services can see basic technical details such as your IP address when your phone connects to them.

What we don't do

  • No ads, and no ad tracking.
  • We never sell or share your information for marketing.
  • No matching with strangers. You only play with people who have your game's code or link.

How we use it

Only to run the game: showing words to the players in your game, revealing rounds, and keeping your list of games. The creator may look at overall totals, like how many games were played in a day, to see how Brainsmith is doing.

Kids

Brainsmith is a family game, and kids play it with their families. It doesn't ask for any personal information beyond a first name or nickname. Parents, if you'd like your child's games removed, contact us and we'll delete them.

Keeping and deleting data

Game records are kept so you can come back to a game later. You can delete your account and data anytime: open Settings (the gear on the start screen) and tap Delete my account. That takes your name out of every game (it shows as "Gone player"), erases your email, sign-in, alerts and blocked list, and can't be undone. The words you played stay in other players' game history, without your name. You can also email us and we'll do it for you.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Big changes, like adding paid features, will be explained here before they start.
